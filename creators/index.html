What is Linkable?

Linkable is a platform where creators discover products they genuinely love and earn commissions for every sale made through their unique links. It’s the easiest way to collaborate with brands that match your content and audience.

How do I get started?

Sign up in seconds, complete your profile, and connect your social accounts. Once approved, you can browse live campaigns, request to promote products, and start earning from your recommendations.

Do I need a large following to join?

Not at all. We work with creators of all sizes as long as your content is authentic and you engage genuinely with your audience. What matters most is your ability to influence, not your follower count.

How do I earn money?

You earn a commission every time someone purchases a product through your Linkable tracking link. Each brand sets its own commission rate, and you’ll always see it upfront before joining a campaign. If your content performs particularly well, brands may also invite you to create or share dedicated content for a fixed payment, giving you the opportunity to earn both from sales and paid collaborations.

When do I get paid?

You can track every sale and payout status directly from your dashboard, giving you full visibility over your earnings in real time. Once your balance is ready for withdrawal, you can receive payments in your preferred currency, offering flexibility no matter where you’re based.

Can I promote more than one brand?

Yes. You can collaborate with multiple brands at the same time, choosing products that best fit your niche and audience.

Do I need to connect social media accounts?

Yes. Linking at least one social media profile allows brands to view your engagement metrics and helps you get matched with the right campaigns.

Is Linkable available worldwide?

Yes. Linkable supports international payouts so you can join and get paid from anywhere it’s available.

How do I earn on Linkable?

As a creator on Linkable, a leading creator collaboration platform, you can connect with brands that align with your content and audience. Once you establish a collaboration, you earn commissions by promoting products through affiliate links. When someone makes a purchase using your unique link, you receive a percentage of the sale. The more products you promote and the more your audience engages, the higher your earning potential.

Are there any requirements before joining Linkable?

How do I turn my Instagram account into a business account?
