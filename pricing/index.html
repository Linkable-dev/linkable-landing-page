Why should I use Linkable for my creator collaborations?

Linkable makes creator partnerships simple, measurable, and secure all in one place. You can connect directly with authentic creators who fit your brand, launch campaigns in minutes, and track every click and sale from one dashboard.

How is Linkable different from other influencer or affiliate platforms?

Linkable was built for ecommerce brands that want transparency and measurable growth. Unlike traditional influencer tools, it integrates directly with your store to show the full picture from creator engagement to real sales. Every collaboration is trackable, every payout is automated, and every partnership is designed to deliver results you can measure.

Can I measure ROI?

Yes. Every campaign you run through Linkable is fully trackable. You’ll see live data on clicks, sales, and commissions, giving you a clear view of how creators impact your bottom line.

How much does it cost?

You can start with a 30-day free trial to explore everything Linkable has to offer. After that, plans start at £99 per month, giving you full access to campaign tools, analytics, and automated payouts.

Do you offer onboarding or support?

Yes. We’ll guide you through setup, help you create your first campaign, and support you along the way. Whether it’s technical guidance or campaign advice, we’re here to make sure you get results from day one.

What makes Linkable different from social commerce platforms?

Platforms like TikTok Shop focus on social selling. Linkable focuses on brand growth through trust, giving you the infrastructure to build ongoing partnerships with creators who already align with your products and values.

What types of brands perform best on Linkable?

Ambitious ecommerce brands that value authenticity and measurable results. From fast-growing Shopify stores to established DTC names, Linkable helps you turn creator collaborations into a consistent sales channel, not just a marketing experiment.

How does it work?

As brands on Linkable, you can easily connect with creators who align with your target audience and brand values. Showcase your products, set commission rates, and collaborate seamlessly through affiliate links. Our platform simplifies performance tracking and partnership management, making collaboration effortless and impactful.

How do I view a creator's social media insights

To view a creator’s performance metrics, go to their profile and click on the analytics section. Our Instagram integration lets you access key insights like follower count, engagement rate, average likes, comments, audience demographics and more. This data helps you evaluate the creator’s reach and find the best fit for your brand.

How can I track the revenue from a Linkable-driven collaboration?

To track your campaign performance, click on your profile icon in the top right corner and select “Insights.” You’ll see a detailed breakdown of clicks and sales, giving you a clear view of your engagement and revenue generated from the collaboration. To track your overall performance, click on your profile icon in the top right corner and select “Insights.” You’ll see a detailed breakdown of clicks and sales, giving you a clear view of your engagement and revenue generated from all collaborations.

When are creators paid?

When one of your promoted products is sold, our platform will automatically deduct our commission from the sale. The remaining funds will then be securely held for the Creator until they are ready to be released at the next payout.
